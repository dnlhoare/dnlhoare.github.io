Two directions, one estimate

Enter a set to estimate its 1RM. The table then works backwards to show loads likely to correspond to each target rep count.

02

How the estimate works

Epley and Brzycki are common rep-max equations. Both become less reliable as reps climb, so this tool caps the input at 15 and highlights the 1–10 rep range.

03

Use your own judgement

These are planning guides. Warm up, use safeties or a spotter where appropriate, and let technique and how you feel set the limit.
