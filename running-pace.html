How to read these estimates

Race times use the Riegel power law: T₂ = T₁ × (D₂ / D₁)1.06. It is a simple equivalency model for endurance efforts, so predictions become less reliable the farther the target is from your result. Sprint estimates (100–800 m) are especially rough.

VDOT uses the Daniels–Gilbert oxygen cost and sustainable-fraction equations, and is only shown for efforts from 1500 m to marathon. It is a performance index sometimes called “effective” VO₂max; it does not equal a measured laboratory VO₂max. Age and weight scenarios are illustrative, broad assumptions. Marathon outcomes also depend heavily on preparation and fueling.

Sources: Riegel, “Athletic Records and Human Endurance” · Daniels–Gilbert VDOT equations · Review of age related VO₂ changes.
